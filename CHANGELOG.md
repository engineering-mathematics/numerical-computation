### Added
- Root finding: `bisect`, `brent`, `newton`, `solve_system` (damped Newton using conditioning-checked solves).

- Quadrature: `integrate_function`, `trapezoid`, `simpson`.
- Interpolation: `Interpolant` with linear, cubic-spline and PCHIP kinds.
- Typed results and errors.
